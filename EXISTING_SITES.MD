# Existing Sites for Reference

Simple, image-first furniture and woodworking sites, collected as design references for SOSART, Arte en Madera. See [FURNITURE.md](FURNITURE.md) and [STRATEGY.md](STRATEGY.md).

These come from web search summaries. The sites themselves have not been reviewed, so check each one before relying on it.

## Sites

| Site | Why it is relevant |
| --- | --- |
| [Croft House](https://freshysites.com/web-design-development/top-custom-furniture-websites/) | Short homepage led by polished images. The only text is in the links. |
| [The Joinery](https://freshysites.com/web-design-development/best-woodworking-websites/) | Clean layout with a high-resolution photo of each piece and a short story paragraph. Closest to the provenance idea. |

| [Vorrath Woodworks](https://freshysites.com/web-design-development/best-woodworking-websites/) | Minimal, with large photos and a five-item menu (about, portfolio, capabilities, careers, contact). |

| Rotsen Furniture | Wooden pieces on a plain white background. Found in the [HubSpot roundup](https://blog.hubspot.com/website/furniture-web-design). |

## Patterns to Borrow

- Keep the homepage to one or two large photos and a short menu.
- Give each piece a short story paragraph next to its photos.
- Use plenty of white space and let the wood provide the color.
- Use a centered menu that goes straight into the collection.

## Gaps

Almost all of these are US or European makers. None is known to be bilingual or built around a WhatsApp contact, which this project needs.
