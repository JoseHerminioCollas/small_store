# Phase 1 Work Estimate

Rough estimate for one developer who knows JavaScript, using Claude Code to speed up the coding. Astro and Decap CMS are assumed to be new, so learning time is included. See [STRATEGY.md](STRATEGY.md) for the approach and [FURNITURE.md](FURNITURE.md) for the client specifics.

## Development

| Task | Hours |
| --- | --- |
| Astro scaffold, ES/EN routing, layout, header with language switcher and WhatsApp button | 4-6 |
| Content collection for products, and the Home, Collection, Product, About, Visit and Contact pages | 8-12 |
| Product gallery (main image, thumbnails, mobile swipe, lightbox) | 4-6 |
| Styling and responsive design, including choosing type, colors and layout to suit the wood | 8-12 |
| Decap CMS setup, with bilingual fields and image lists, and Netlify auth | 4-6 |
| Contact form, Google Map embed, SEO basics (titles, descriptions, sitemap, alt text) | 3-4 |
| Domain purchase, DNS, deploy to Netlify, analytics | 2-3 |
| Testing on phones and browsers, and fixing what turns up | 3-4 |
| Client CMS training, and handover notes | 2-3 |
| **Total** | **about 38-56 hours** |

## Work Outside Development

| Item | Estimate |
| --- | --- |
| Photography: 6-12 pieces with 5 photos each (30-60 shots) plus editing | 1-2 days, more if lighting needs setting up |
| Copy: bilingual text for each page and product, plus a native English review | 4-8 hours, mostly waiting on the client to answer questions about their process and wood |
| Google Business Profile | About 1 hour, plus a few days for Google to verify the address by postcard or phone |

## Calendar Time
- About 1-2 weeks of steady part-time work (roughly 20 hours a week) for the build.
- Photos and copy are usually the bottleneck. Build with placeholder content in parallel, and drop in the real content through the CMS later.
- Realistically, expect 3-4 weeks from start to launch.
- The client's review and feedback cycle can add about a week.

## What Moves the Estimate
- **Design:** a simple, clean design that lets the photos carry the site keeps the estimate at the low end. Custom animation and heavy branding work are where it grows most.
- **Photo readiness:** having photos and copy ready at the start removes most of the waiting.
- **CMS auth:** the estimate assumes Netlify with Decap. Hosting elsewhere adds an OAuth setup.
- **Languages:** each extra language adds copy, review and CMS fields.
- **Client feedback:** the number of revision rounds is the biggest unknown.

## Assumptions
- One developer, part time, with no prior Astro or Decap experience.
- No custom branding work: the logo and business cards already exist.
- No e-commerce in phase 1 (see Phase 2 in STRATEGY.md).
- Hours are estimates, not quotes. Revisit them after the scaffold and first product page are built, since that will show the real pace.
